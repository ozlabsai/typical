Typically

Your own Typical, taught from the decisions you already made.

Upload a spreadsheet of past cases and what you decided. We build a version of Typical that decides the way you do, then show it next to the standard one.

Step 1

Bring your past decisions.

A spreadsheet saved as CSV: one row per case, one column describing the case, and one or more columns with what you decided.

Step 2

Say which decisions to learn.

Pick the column that describes each case. For every other column you want Typical to learn, tick it and word the question the way you would ask it.

Decisions

Step 3

Teach it.

We split your history into examples to learn from and cases we keep back to test it on, and make sure rare answers are not drowned out by common ones.

Training takes ~10 minutes on one GPU. Run this on the machine that has it:

Try it.
Paste a new case. Both models read it and say how likely each answer is. The likeliest answer is in bold.
