# R054 plan: train on what the benchmark scores, at a real dose

Date: 2026-10-08. Evidence: `.context/research-review/{LEDGER,PER_QUESTION_GAP,LIQUID_AI,BASE_AND_LEADERS,SYNTHESIS,REDTEAM}.md`.

## Diagnosis
- About 30 Gemma adapters never really changed the base. They used LoRA r32, lr 5e-6–2e-5, 800–3,600 presentations each, single seeds, and were judged on 128-row panels. R044's per-question answers correlate .85–.97 with the frozen base, and its WorkflowEvals mean is .528 against the base's .521.
- They trained toward the wrong target. Gold = `workflow_program(mean(gpt-6-astra, claude-fable-5-1) probabilities)`, with thresholds inside (0,1). We trained on executable hard gold, which teaches confident answers. The gold rewards placing a probability in the right *band*.
- About 7 of the 15-point gap is the calibration of a single security question. Holding `is_true_positive` at a constant 0.3–0.55 already scores security .583, against R044's .338 and Jev's .617. The remaining ~8 points are judgment errors on agent_trace, invoice and customer, mostly on choice/score fields.
- JevBench is nearly solved by the base: frozen Gemma (Cygnet) scores 73.70 against Jev's 72.13 and needs only +0.9 Intelligence. Reasoning at serve time hurts JevBench through latency and cost, so any reasoning has to be distilled into the single pass.
- EmbeddingGemma 2 or other encoder bases (Intelligence ≤21 on the board), LFM/d1 bases (no Hebrew), and 27B+ students (they fail JevBench's cost axis) are all rejected. Larger models are teachers only.

## Phase 1 — distillation at real dose, plus one new loss
- **Data:** open-source and generated workflow-shaped states, contamination-filtered, roughly 3–5k cases giving ~50k questions. They are labeled by the best open thinking teacher from P0, with ≤300 frontier labels as an anchor. No benchmark cases are used. We disclose that the four public workflow schemas are reused, and hold out 2–3 new schemas as a transfer panel.
- **Student:** a fresh Gemma-4-12B-it with LoRA r64, lr 1e-4, ~50k presentations, 16k context. Hebrew and image replay rows are trained with KL to the frozen base.
- **Arms (2 seeds each):**
  - (A) KL to the teacher mean
  - (B) A + **Pivotal Threshold Distillation**: a band loss applied only at the thresholds where crossing changes the program's action set. It is first tested for free on cached hidden states and advances only if it wins by ≥2 points.
- **Calibration:** per-type temperatures fitted on held-out teacher-labeled synthetic data, never on the benchmark.
- **Gate:** at least +6 WorkflowEvals points over the frozen base on both seeds, security ≥ .50, public JevBench ≥ 199/231, and Hebrew/image within 2/128 of the base.

## Phase 2
GPT-6-astra anchor labels (when Codex resets on Oct 14, or after an OpenRouter top-up) to cover agent_trace. Then average the seeds and blend toward the base, run one final WorkflowEvals, and make the official JevBench submission. Stop if WorkflowEvals < .64 or any workflow is more than 5 points under Jev's row. Aim for about +4 points per workflow, because "each workflow ≥ Jev" is noisy.

## Not now (and why)
- **RL:** the single-pass distribution is differentiable, so RL adds noise without adding information.
- **Joint multi-question head:** the gold is built per question.
- **Attention/KDA surgery:** no evidence it is the bottleneck.
- **Serve-time thinking:** JevBench latency and cost penalties.
