## Why it was rejected

The claimed value/operator holdout was nominal. `_make_fact` stored the split-exclusive
`value_regime` and `operator_composition` strings, but neither changed the generated operands;
`_fact_truth` ignored both. The semantic hash included those strings plus split-exclusive
renderer/source names, so the reported zero overlap did not prove executable novelty.

After removing decorative family tags, all 50 calibration latent cases occurred in training.
Evaluation contained 98 policy-bearing signatures, but only 70 were novel relative to training;
because the encoder never sees the external policy, only 54 evaluation predicate cases were novel.
The surface-family schedule was also confounded with field-count parity, and causal pivots appeared
only at the first or last field position.

The scorer's `independent_clusters=250` label was therefore not an inference count. Statistical
comparison must use reconstructed semantic signatures; R049 comparator v3 does so.

## Required v1.5 acceptance conditions

- Regime changes must alter the truth-bearing operand schema and evaluator.
- Operator changes must select executable, pairwise-distinct truth functions with explicit
  separating witnesses.
- Semantic signatures must be reconstructed from executable cases and policy partitions, excluding
  renderer/source names and nominal family labels.
- Encoder-case, policy-bearing, grammar, and surface identities must be reported separately.
- Semantic factors must be deterministically crossed rather than inferred from decorated inputs;
  causal pivots must cover interior positions.
- Every eight-view English/Hebrew, option-order, counterfactual cluster must remain one independent
  case, with exact serialization, fuzzy-overlap, and policy-witness guards preserved.

No GPU result may use v1.4 as a clean advancement receipt.
