Voice

Short claims. Present tense. A period when it's a statement.

Headlines M

  • 4–8 words, declarative, present tense: "Edit reality as it happens", "What Live AI unlocks".
  • Full-sentence statements end with a period: "One stack powers every layer of Live AI."
  • Product names get a full stop when introduced: "Decart Optimization Stack."
  • "Live AI" is capitalized like a proper noun. "Speed of reality" is the recurring motif.
  • Numbers are concrete: "up to 100×", "30 FPS", "0.01–0.04% overhead".

Buttons & labels M

  • Verb first, 1–3 words: "Learn more", "Access demo", "Contact us", "Try Lucy 2.5".
  • Links read "Read more →" with a real arrow character.
  • Kickers are one word or a short phrase in grey: "Introducing", "The Live AI Lab".
  • Inline labels end with a colon: "Built across:".
  • Case drifts: home uses sentence case ("Learn more"), /lucy uses Title Case ("Talk To An Expert"). Use sentence case. I

Body copy M

  • Two sentences: the claim, then a short reframe. "Run optimized workloads… Your workload shouldn't care what it runs on."
  • Staccato triplets for emphasis: "No new racks. No new contracts. New ceiling."
  • Second person ("you already have") over "users".

Avoid I

  • Exclamation marks, emoji, questions as headlines (one exception on the blog: "What if video editing had a volume knob?").
  • Hedged claims ("may help", "aims to").
  • Uppercase anywhere but the footer and legal line.

Do / Don't

Check before you ship.

Do

  • One primary blue pill per view; pair it with a secondary outline pill.
  • Headlines at weight 400 with negative tracking.
  • Put a grey kicker above an ink title when a section needs a label.
  • Group things in #f5f5f5 fills with 24–56px radii.
  • Use white sections; let the blue-tint wash appear only behind a statement.
  • Keep hover changes to color/background at 0.2s ease.

Don't

  • Bold headlines (600/700) or positive tracking on large type.
  • Hairline borders around cards, or drop shadows on cards.
  • Square or small-radius buttons.
  • A second accent hue for UI. Green and lavender exist only inside product washes.
  • Hover lifts, scale on buttons, bounce or spring easing.
  • Invent a dark theme — the site has none. Dark appears only as the footer and video heroes.
