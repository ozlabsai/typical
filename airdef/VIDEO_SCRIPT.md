# Typical · Air defence: silent demo video (2:30)

A video that explains itself with no voice-over. Title cards and short captions carry the story, and the screen
proves it. Every moment below is recorded on the hosted demo (typical-airdefence.ozlabs.ai), and it plays the same
way every time, so each shot can be re-taken until it is right.

**The one idea:** for every incoming threat, Typical picks which interceptor and when, and asks again as the
picture changes. The money saved is the proof.

**Rules for every caption:**
- At most 10 words, on screen for at least 3 seconds.
- One caption at a time, never over the panel text it is pointing at.
- Captions state what happens. They never explain the software.

## How to record it

1. **Setup.** Chrome at 1920×1080 (or record a 1680×1000 window and scale up). English UI, page zoom 100%, no
   bookmarks bar. Hide the cursor, or use a soft highlight cursor.
2. **One take per act.** Red Sea (shots 3–7), Northern front (9–11), Drill (12). Reload the page between acts so
   each one starts clean.
3. **Landing on a moment.** Every moment has a marker on the timeline at the bottom of the screen. Clicking a
   marker replays the raid from that moment, so shots 9 and 10 can be re-taken until they are clean. Shots 4–6
   arrive by themselves after Play.
4. **Pacing.** Focus runs the clock itself: it slows down for each story and fast-forwards between them. Keep those
   fast-forwards short in the edit (cut or ramp them); keep each story at its own pace.
5. **Captions.** Figtree 500 in white on a near-black 80% plate, 28–32 px at 1080p, with a fade of about 0.3 s.
   Put them top-left over the map, never over the right-hand panel: the panel is the evidence.
6. **Sound.** A quiet, steady bed (low strings or a soft pulse), no sound effects. Duck it slightly under each
   title card.
7. **Hebrew cut.** Every caption has a Hebrew line in the same place. Switch the UI with the language button and
   record the same acts again; the moments are identical.

## Why it is built this way

- **It opens on the question, not the product.** A viewer with no context needs to know what is being decided
  before they see who decides it.
- **Each act has one outcome and the numbers that prove it.** Red Sea: every threat stopped. Northern: the money
  kept. Drill: the best option, shown as the best.
- **Sending nothing is the strongest silent shot.** A salvo let fall, with no interceptor spent on it, is the
  decision a viewer would not expect from a machine.
- **October is left out on purpose.** It is the saturation stress case and ends in hits; keep it for live
  questions.
